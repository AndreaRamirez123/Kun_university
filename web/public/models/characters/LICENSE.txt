Personajes 3D del campus
========================

Autor: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com)
Packs: "Ultimate Modular Men Pack" y "Ultimate Modular Women Pack"
Licencia: CC0 1.0 Universal (dominio público)

Modificaciones: se conservaron solo las animaciones Idle, Walk, Run, Wave,
Interact y Roll, y se convirtieron a .glb con compresión meshopt
(gltf-transform resample + prune + meshopt).

  man-hoodie.glb       <- Men pack, Casual_Hoodie
  other-astronaut.glb  <- Men pack, Spacesuit
  woman-casual.glb     <- Women pack, Casual
  woman-suit.glb       <- Women pack, Suit
  other-scifi.glb      <- Women pack, SciFi
